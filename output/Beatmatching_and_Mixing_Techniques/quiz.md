### Question 1 (Easy)

**Type**: multiple_choice

What does the term 'beat' refer to in music when discussing DJ techniques?

- A repetition of a melodic phrase
- The basic unit of time or pulse in music
- The speed at which a song plays
- A transition effect between two tracks

**Answer**: The basic unit of time or pulse in music
**Explanation**: The 'beat' is the foundational pulse or basic unit of time in music, crucial for timing and syncing tracks when beatmatching.

Which device feature allows a DJ to adjust the tempo of a track to match another track's speed?

- Crossfader
- Pitch fader

### Question 4 (Medium)

**Type**: multiple_choice

When preparing to start the next track in your headphones while the current track plays through the speakers, this process is known as:

- Mixing
- Cueing
- Scratching
- Looping

**Answer**: Cueing
**Explanation**: Cueing involves isolating and listening to the incoming track via headphones privately while the current track plays publicly, to prepare for beatmatching and transitioning.
*Tests*: Explain headphone and cueing techniques for preparing mixes

### Question 5 (Medium)

**Type**: multiple_choice

Which type of mixing involves gradually adjusting the volume of one track down while increasing the volume of another for a smooth transition?

- Cut mixing
- Fade mixing
- Filter mixing
- Scratch mixing

**Answer**: Fade mixing
**Explanation**: Fade mixing crossfades audio gradually between two tracks to maintain flow and minimize abrupt changes.

## Study Tips
Focus on understanding basic rhythm concepts such as beat, tempo, phrasing first. Practice identifying beats and downbeats in different music genres using active listening. Then, familiarize yourself with DJ equipment controls like pitch faders and jog wheels through hands-on practice or simulations. Study transition types and EQ adjustments, and review the purpose and proper use of effects. Finally, plan and review your mixes by recording and analyzing your performances to improve timing and smoothness.

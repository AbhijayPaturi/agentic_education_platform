## Questions

### Question 1 (Easy)

**Type**: multiple_choice

Which business structure offers limited liability protection for DJs while allowing pass-through taxation?

- Sole Proprietorship
- Partnership
- Limited Liability Company (LLC)
- Corporation

**Answer**: Limited Liability Company (LLC)
**Explanation**: LLCs provide limited liability protection for owners while allowing profits and losses to be passed through to owners' personal tax returns, avoiding double taxation.
*Tests*: Understand DJ business structures

---

### Question 2 (Easy)

**Type**: true_false

Registering a business name as a DBA (Doing Business As) protects the business name legally from being used by others.

**Answer**: False
**Explanation**: A DBA registration allows a business to operate under a different name but does not provide exclusive trademark protection for that name.
*Tests*: Understand business naming and trademark basics

### Question 3 (Medium)

**Type**: multiple_choice

What is the primary purpose of a performance license for DJs?

- To allow use of copyrighted music publicly
- To avoid paying taxes
- To register the DJ as a business entity
- To sell music tracks legally

**Answer**: To allow use of copyrighted music publicly
**Explanation**: A performance license permits the public performance of copyrighted music legally, which is essential for DJs playing music at events.
*Tests*: Understand music licensing requirements

---

### Question 4 (Easy)

**Type**: short_answer

Name one major performing rights organization (PRO) that DJs might need to work with.

**Answer**: ASCAP (or BMI or SESAC)
**Explanation**: Major PROs like ASCAP, BMI, and SESAC manage performance rights and licensing for songwriters and publishers, which DJs must consider.
*Tests*: Identify major PROs for music licensing

Which tax form should a self-employed DJ typically use to report income?

- W-2

- Form SS-4

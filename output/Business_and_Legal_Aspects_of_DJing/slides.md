## Copyright Basics in DJing

*Type: bullet_points*

- Copyright grants exclusive rights to creators over their music.
- Protects the right to reproduce, distribute, perform, and create derivative works.
- Applies to recordings, compositions, and arrangements.
- Violating copyright can result in legal penalties.

*Example:* Using a track without permission in a public DJ set may breach copyright laws.

> **Notes**: Explain copyright purpose clearly, referencing music as intellectual property. Use simple analogies, e.g., comparing to owning a unique painting.

---

## Music Licensing Types & Public Performance Rights

*Type: bullet_points*

- **Performance Rights Organizations (PROs):** ASCAP, BMI, SESAC license public performance rights.
- **Types of Licenses:** Blanket licenses, synchronization licenses, mechanical licenses.
- Venues usually acquire blanket licenses covering DJs’ performances.
- DJs must understand licensing to avoid unauthorized use.

*Example:* A nightclub typically holds a blanket license allowing DJs to legally play copyrighted music.

> **Notes**: Clarify how licensing allows public use of copyrighted music legally. Stress the interaction between DJs, venues, and PROs.

## Fair Use & Sampling in DJing

*Type: text*

Fair use is a limited exception allowing certain uses of copyrighted works without permission, e.g., commentary or parody. However, sampling music in DJ sets often requires permission unless the sample is very short or altered. Misusing samples can lead to copyright infringement.

Understanding boundaries helps DJs creatively incorporate samples while respecting rights.

> **Notes**: Use examples of famous DJs who sample legally or faced lawsuits due to unauthorized sampling. Emphasize caution in sample use.

## Understanding DJ Contracts: Structure & Language

*Type: bullet_points*

- Contracts formalize agreements between DJs and clients or agencies.
- Common contract components:
  - Parties involved
  - Description of services
  - Payment terms and schedule
  - Cancellation and liability clauses
- Clear, simple language reduces misunderstandings.

> **Notes**: Highlight the importance of reading every contract detail. Use analogies like contracts being a roadmap for the DJ-client relationship.

---

## Types of DJ Contracts

*Type: bullet_points*

- **Gig Contracts:** Specific to individual events.
- **Agency Agreements:** Define representation and booking arrangements.
- **Sponsorship Contracts:** Outline endorsements and promotional activities.

Each type has unique clauses and obligations DJs must review carefully.

> **Notes**: Clarify differences and when each contract type applies. Use examples like festival gigs vs. brand sponsorships.

## Key Contract Clauses & Negotiation Basics

*Type: bullet_points*

- **Payment terms:** Amount, timing, method.
- **Cancellation policies:** Fees for cancellations by either party.
- **Liabilities:** Handling damages, injuries, or equipment loss.
- **Exclusivity:** Restrictions on performing elsewhere.

*Negotiation Tip:* Understand your value and be prepared to discuss terms clearly.

> **Notes**: Use a case study or scenario illustrating how a clause can impact a DJ. Coach learners on negotiation as a dialogue, not confrontation.

---

## Common Contract Pitfalls & Red Flags

*Type: bullet_points*

- Vague or incomplete terms.
- Unreasonable cancellation penalties.
- Lack of clarity on payment deadlines.
- Hidden exclusivity or non-compete clauses.
- Lack of indemnity or liability protections for the DJ.

> **Notes**: Encourage vigilance and consulting with professionals if unsure. Share anecdotes where ignoring red flags led to issues.

## Obtaining Music Performance Licenses

*Type: bullet_points*

- Determine if the venue holds appropriate licenses.
- Apply for licenses if performing at unlicensed locations.
- Contact PROs for licensing guidance.
- Keep records of licenses and permissions.

*Scenario:* Performing at a private party often requires different licensing than a public commercial event.

> **Notes**: Use role-play scenarios to help learners assess licensing needs in various settings.

## Legal Implications of Online Streaming & Recorded Sets

*Type: bullet_points*

- Streaming performances may require additional licenses.
- Distributing recorded mixes must respect copyright and licensing.
- Using platforms’ built-in licensing can help but verify coverage.
- Always credit original artists when possible.
